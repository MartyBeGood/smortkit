Invoke the **review** skill (`skills/review/SKILL.md`). Construct a senior
reviewer whose authority comes from the codebase, §R, and live best-practice,
then try to refute the active initiative's spec (§G §C §I §R §V §T), checked
against `CONSTITUTION.md`. Every finding cites evidence — file:line or
source; flag unverifiable ones `[unverified]`. Draft local §V lines for
HARDEN findings and hand them to spec. Propose the rare project-wide §V as a
promotion candidate to `CONSTITUTION.md` §V — review is the only path there.
End on an explicit GO / NO-GO gate.
